The VA loan is different.

$0 down options*

Eligible veterans can often buy without a down payment, so your savings can go toward everything else.

No monthly mortgage insurance

Many low-down-payment loans charge it every month. VA loans don't.

More room on credit than most people assume

The VA doesn't set a minimum score. Lenders set their own, so it's worth asking before you decide you're not ready.

Your VA loan can do more than buy a house.
